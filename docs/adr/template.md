# NNNN. Title of the decision

## Status

Proposed | Accepted | Superseded by [NNNN](./NNNN-other-decision.md) | Deprecated

## Context

What problem or constraint forced a decision? What forces (technical,
organizational, external) are in tension? State facts, not the decision
itself — a future reader should be able to reach a different conclusion
from the same context and understand why they didn't.

## Decision

What was decided, stated as a plain declarative sentence ("We will ..."),
plus enough detail that someone could implement it without re-deriving it.

## Consequences

What becomes easier or harder as a result. Include the tradeoffs accepted,
not just the upside — an ADR that only lists benefits isn't useful when
someone later hits the downside and wonders if it was considered.
